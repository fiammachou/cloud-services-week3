• Describe reasons why so many (most?) companies are using cloud based email and calendar services

The use of cloud-based services such as email and calendars offers many advantages for today’s businesses, such as accessibility: it is much easier to access these services from different devices, meaning they can be used at any time. Furthermore, there is scalability, which allows you to tailor costs, speed and storage to your needs, thereby avoiding the waste of resources, as well as eliminating the initial infrastructure costs, such as hardware and system maintenance.

• Your thoughts about when to maintain your own web server and when to use SaaS/PaaS/IaaS to provide web services?

This highlights the fundamental difference between cloud computing and traditional IT. In traditional IT, organisations use IT assets by purchasing, installing, managing and maintaining them in on-premises data centres. With cloud computing, the cloud service provider owns, manages and maintains the assets. The customer uses them via an internet connection and pays for them through a subscription or on a pay-as-you-go basis.

• Describe reasons, why many IoT devices (internet connected home appliances, Smart-TVs and such) are using Google's 8.8.8.8 or some other public DNS resolvers?

IoT devices use 8.8.8.8 to ensure they can always connect to their servers, thereby avoiding the frequent failures and outages of local internet service providers’ DNS servers. Furthermore, configuring a standard public DNS globally enables manufacturers to simplify technical support and circumvent security blocks on home routers that might prevent their applications from functioning.
